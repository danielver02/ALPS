public subroutine cache_load(directory, id, gamma, parallel, distribution, derivatives, normalization, hit, reason)

Transactional load: rejected entries never modify caller arrays. Arrays retain zero-based target-grid bounds; derivative component bounds are 1:2. On hit, normalization is the saved pre-division integral, not a new integration. The caller must use an identity from cache_identity and matching destination shapes.
