No support/unit-disk cutoff. Failure outputs are quiet NaNs, not zeros. Checked arithmetic works even when the caller enables overflow traps. Quiet NaNs/infinities are rejected; signaling NaNs may raise IEEE invalid before a status is returned. The caller's FP environment is never changed.
