public subroutine build_rel_cheb_rows(gamma, parallel, distribution, species, degrees, status, selected, diagnostic_unit)

Explicit initialization entry: invoke on rank 0 after derivative_f0 has loaded/constructed and normalized the transformed tables. The conditional initialization hook is shared by supported production and validation input. species maps packed slots to original species; degrees=poly_order(species). selected optionally restricts applicable slots, without changing packing. Rebuilding first releases this collection only. Inputs are never modified. SUCCESS means construction completed without unexpected row failures; expected empty/zero rows still have unavailable models and explicit states. private subroutine cheb_fit_direct(u, values, requested_degree, model, status, rcond)

Unweighted rectangular SVD fit on actual, possibly repeated/unsorted nodes. The default RCOND is max(number of samples,initial columns)*epsilon. A degenerate interval has center=u(1), scale=1 and is fitted as a constant. Rank loss causes a new lower-degree least-squares solve, never truncation. Input contract (also cheb_evaluate): finite data are supported; quiet NaNs and infinities are rejected by status. Signaling NaNs are not trap-safe: they may raise IEEE invalid before a status can be returned. No FP modes or exception traps are changed by this kernel.
